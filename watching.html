Worth a detour

50 websites to explore

From aircraft over Sydney and ships at sea to old maps, art collections and music from another decade, these are places to follow a curiosity for a few minutes — or disappear into for an afternoon. Pick a subject and see where it takes you.

Some sites offer paid extras or ask for an account. Live maps depend on coverage and availability; rolling statistics are often estimates. Each link opens the original site in a new tab.
