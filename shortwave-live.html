Where to tune first

Propagation is never guaranteed. The highlighted bands are simply the ones that usually make the most sense for the current part of the day.

Military / government HF

HFGCS voice frequencies

Common US High Frequency Global Communications System channels. You may hear routine aircraft calls, ground stations or coded Emergency Action Messages.

11175kHz USB

The most commonly monitored HFGCS frequency and a sensible first channel to try.

8992kHz USB

Often useful after dark or when the lower HF path is stronger than 11175.

15016kHz USB

A higher-frequency HFGCS channel that can be worth checking during daylight.
