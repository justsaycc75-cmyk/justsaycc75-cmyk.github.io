War Watch Intel says the latest hour remains quiet — but the radio traffic is worth watching

War Watch Intel’s latest hourly update reports six notable military aircraft in the air and 14 coded U.S. Air Force radio broadcasts heard today, while describing the areas it watches as being at normal activity levels.

That makes this a useful companion to the developing Middle East story below. The service tracks military aircraft, HFGCS Emergency Action Messages and open-source reporting around the clock. For shortwave listeners, HFGCS traffic is worth monitoring because it can show changes in communications activity — but the coded messages do not reveal their meaning and are not, by themselves, confirmation that a military operation is imminent.
