Heartbreak at the SCG as Fremantle runs down the Swans

Sydney led by 28 points during the third quarter and looked headed for another Grand Final. Then the game turned completely.

The Dockers kicked five goals to none in the final term to win by 12 points, 12.11 (83) to 10.11 (71). Sydney still held a 21-point advantage at three-quarter time, but Fremantle overwhelmed the Swans late, winning the last-quarter inside-50 count 23–6 and the clearances 12–5.

Errol Gulden finished with 36 disposals and Logan McDonald kicked three goals for Sydney. Hayden Young's goal after the three-quarter-time siren gave Fremantle belief, before Shai Bolton, Patrick Voss and Mason Cox helped turn the match in the final term.

The season ends one win short of the Grand Final. Sydney's finals run had gathered serious momentum, but the late collapse at home will sting for a long time.
