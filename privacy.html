Visitor privacy

This site uses a small invisible image from Stats4U to count visits. It does not set cookies or ask visitors to sign in. The site owner receives daily estimates of page views and distinct visitors, with recognised bots shown separately.

Loading the image sends your IP address, browser identification and the site address to Stats4U as part of the web request. Stats4U uses a changing daily hash to estimate distinct visitors; its server access logs may keep an IP address for up to 14 days, and aggregate statistics for up to 400 days. The counter's image-only format does not measure clicks, scrolls or screen size.

Counts are estimates. A person using several devices may be counted more than once, the owner's visits may be included, and some bots may not be recognised. For more detail, see Stats4U's privacy policy.
