NRL + NFL

Sport

Four teams. Two codes. One page to keep track of what actually matters.

This is not meant to be a general sports-news page. It is the short list: the Sydney Roosters in the NRL, plus the New York Giants, Seattle Seahawks and Green Bay Packers in the NFL. The idea is to make it obvious, at a glance, who is playing next, where the game is, what has changed during the week and which players are worth watching.

During the season this page will concentrate on the next match for each team, important injuries and team changes, the result of the previous game, standout performers and any story that genuinely changes the outlook. In other words: useful context, not fifty paragraphs of filler.

Four teams, one weekly scoreboard

This is the home base for the Sydney Roosters, New York Giants, Seattle Seahawks and Green Bay Packers. Each week I can drop the latest result in here so the page builds into a simple season diary — who they played, the score, where it happened and the main talking point from the game.

Over time it becomes a quick record of the season rather than just a list of upcoming fixtures.

Results worth remembering

Each team can have its own running line of results, with wins and losses easy to scan. Big performances, close finishes, blowouts, surprise wins and important injuries can all be noted alongside the score.

The idea is simple: four teams, every result, and just enough context to remember what happened without turning it into a sports newspaper.
