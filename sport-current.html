2026 premiers: Roosters edge the Knights 19–18.

Image: NRL.com match footage
FULL TIME • 19–18ROOSTERS PREMIERS
NRL • Grand Final result • Sunday 4 October 2026

Roosters hold on in a one-point Grand Final thriller.

The Sydney Roosters are the 2026 NRL premiers after defeating the Newcastle Knights 19–18 at Accor Stadium on Sunday 4 October. Daly Cherry-Evans’ field goal proved the difference in a gripping decider.

James Tedesco capped the win with the Clive Churchill Medal. The Roosters captain made 14 tackle breaks and ran for 385 metres, then helped secure the result with two crucial late takes under pressure.

Newcastle fought to the final whistle despite having only 43 per cent of possession. The Knights’ remarkable run from last place in 2025 ended just one point short of the premiership.

Sydney Roosters

Premiership nights that made history

NRL • 2018 & 2019
▶
2018 NRL Grand Final • Roosters 21–6 Storm

Cronk’s broken shoulder, Keary’s masterpiece

The Roosters blew Melbourne away early, led 18–0 at halftime and shut down the defending premiers. Cooper Cronk played through a fractured scapula while Luke Keary controlled the game and won the Clive Churchill Medal.

▶
2019 NRL Grand Final • Roosters 14–8 Raiders

The first NRL-era back-to-back premiers

A tense decider turned on a late James Tedesco try. The win made the Roosters the first club in the NRL era to successfully defend a premiership and sealed one of the modern game’s great two-year runs.

New York Giants

Twice shocking New England

NFL • Super Bowls XLII & XLVI
▶
Super Bowl XLII • Giants 17–14 Patriots

The upset that stopped perfection

New England arrived 18–0, but the Giants’ pass rush kept Tom Brady under pressure and Eli Manning’s escape-and-throw to David Tyree set up the winning touchdown to Plaxico Burress with 35 seconds left.

▶
Super Bowl XLVI • Giants 21–17 Patriots

Manningham on the sideline, Bradshaw at the finish

Four years later the Giants did it again. Mario Manningham’s remarkable sideline catch launched the decisive fourth-quarter drive before Ahmad Bradshaw’s late touchdown gave New York another championship over New England.

Seattle Seahawks

The Legion of Boom at full force

NFL • 2013 & 2014 seasons
▶
Super Bowl XLVIII • Seahawks 43–8 Broncos

Seattle dismantles the league’s best offence

The game began with a safety on Denver’s first snap and never changed direction. Seattle’s defence overwhelmed Peyton Manning and the highest-scoring offence in NFL history to win the franchise’s first Super Bowl.

▶
2014 NFC Championship • Seahawks 28–22 Packers OT

One of the wildest comebacks in playoff history

Seattle trailed 16–0 at halftime and 19–7 with only minutes remaining. A fake field goal, recovered onside kick, two-point conversion and Russell Wilson’s overtime touchdown pass sent the Seahawks back to the Super Bowl.

Green Bay Packers

From the Ice Bowl to Rodgers

NFL • 1967 & 2010 seasons
▶
1967 NFL Championship • Packers 21–17 Cowboys

The Ice Bowl

Played at Lambeau Field in brutal sub-zero conditions, Green Bay drove 68 yards in the final minutes before Bart Starr’s quarterback sneak with 13 seconds left delivered Vince Lombardi’s third straight NFL championship.

▶
Super Bowl XLV • Packers 31–25 Steelers

Rodgers brings the Lombardi Trophy home

Aaron Rodgers threw three touchdown passes and Green Bay forced three Pittsburgh turnovers. The Packers survived a second-half comeback to claim their fourth Super Bowl and 13th NFL championship.

Founded 190816 premiershipsAllianz Stadium

One of Australian rugby league’s foundation clubs and the only one to have played every top-flight season since 1908. Easts won their first premiership in 1911, built famous dynasties in the 1930s and 1970s, and became the first NRL-era side to go back-to-back in 2018 and 2019.

Names woven into the club: Dally Messenger, Arthur Beetson, Brad Fittler.

Founded 19258 championshipsMetLife Stadium

A century-old NFL cornerstone. The Giants won four titles before the Super Bowl era and four more after it, with championships in 1986, 1990, 2007 and 2011. Their history runs from the Polo Grounds to MetLife and from bruising defence to two famous Super Bowl wins over New England.

Names woven into the club: Lawrence Taylor, Phil Simms, Michael Strahan, Eli Manning.

NFL • SeattleSeattle Seahawks
Founded 1976Super Bowl XLVIIILumen Field

Seattle arrived in the NFL in 1976 and grew into one of the league’s loudest, most distinctive franchises. The peak came with the Legion of Boom era and a crushing 43–8 win over Denver in Super Bowl XLVIII. The noise, the 12s and Lumen Field are a big part of the identity.

Names woven into the club: Steve Largent, Marshawn Lynch, Richard Sherman, Kam Chancellor.

Founded 191913 championshipsLambeau Field

The small-city giant of American football. Green Bay was formed in 1919, became community-owned in 1923 and remains the NFL’s only publicly owned franchise. No club has won more NFL championships, and the Lombardi era helped turn the Packers into one of the sport’s defining teams.

Names woven into the club: Curly Lambeau, Vince Lombardi, Bart Starr, Brett Favre, Aaron Rodgers.
