Our story

Write as much or as little as you like, and add your own holiday photos. Drafts are saved on this device only. Nothing is published automatically.

Photos are resized for the web without cropping. Up to 12 photos per story.

Publish through GitHub

To publish, download your story file, open the GitHub upload page, upload the file and commit it. For an existing story, replace the same filename. Your public journal will then display it. GitHub requires your signed-in account. Keep a backup of drafts before clearing browser storage.
